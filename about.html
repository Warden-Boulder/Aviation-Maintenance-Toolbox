About The Toolbox


This toolbox is designed with aircraft mechanics in mind while they’re working across various airframes. With that in mind, it is intended to serve as a supplemental aid to the mechanic(s) out in the field and/or hangar and not to be a replacement for any of the required tooling, manuals, or procedures outlined by official documentation or company SOPs. All results should be verified against current approved maintenance data by the mechanic(s) performing the work.

The toolbox originally began as a Python-based program developed while working on both Boeing 737NG and 737 Max aircraft. Over time it evolved into the web-based toolkit it is today to make these checks more accessible and easier to use in real-world environments in addition to providing additional tools to assist aircraft mechanics.

About Me


I’m an aircraft mechanic by trade with nearly a decade of hands-on experience in aviation maintenance. My experience ranges from various checks/inspections to other scheduled and unscheduled maintenance across both Boeing and Airbus aircraft families. I began learning how to program around 2020 as a way to aid in building my skill set.

My aim is to build practical tools that improve workflow efficiency and make them accessible to others who may find them useful.
